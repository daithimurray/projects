One house. Every way in, covered.

A good system is designed around how your home is actually used. Scroll through a typical install and watch each part do its job.

  1. Door and window contacts

    Zone 1 Front door

    Magnetic contacts on doors and windows. Open one while the system is set and the alarm knows exactly which one.

  2. Motion detectors

    Zone 2 Hall

    PIR detectors cover halls, landings and open-plan rooms. Set the system to guard downstairs only while you sleep upstairs.

  3. CCTV

    Cam 1 Driveway

    Cameras on the driveway, doors and back garden record day and night, so you can see what happened, not just that something did.

  4. Fire detection

    Zone 5 Landing

    Smoke and heat detectors raise the alarm early, whether or not the intruder alarm is set.

  5. Siren and security lighting

    External

    A bell box on the front wall and floodlights on the approach make it plain the house is protected, before and during an alarm.

  6. Alerts on your phone

    HKC app

    On HKC systems, alerts and images reach the app on your phone, wherever you are.

Questions people ask before they call

Still have a question?

Call 01 615 5980Or send the details and we’ll ring you back
Can you upgrade the alarm I already have?

Often, yes, and we can add new doors and windows to it after an extension. Tell us the make and model if you know it, or describe the keypad, and we’ll advise.

What if my alarm goes off in the middle of the night?

Call 01 615 5980. We run a 24/7 emergency call-out, 365 days a year.

Are you licensed?

Yes. Amptech is the trading name of Robert Farnan Electrical & Alarms Limited, licensed by the Private Security Authority under licence number 03019. The licence covers intruder alarms, CCTV and access control.

What do I get when the installation is finished?

A demonstration of the system before we leave. For intruder alarms, you also get a uniquely numbered Certificate of Compliance to EN 50131-1. Your insurer may ask for it, and it may help with your premium.

Can my alarm be monitored?

Yes. We offer 24/7 monitored systems that call your designated keyholders and can carry a Garda Unique Reference Number (URN), so a confirmed alarm can get a Garda response. If you’d rather not pay for monitoring, a GSM unit can text alerts straight to your phone, with no landline and no annual fee.

Can I check my alarm from my phone?

Yes, if it’s an HKC system: the HKC app lets you control and check it from anywhere. Have a different make? Ask us about an upgrade, or about a GSM unit that texts alerts to your phone.

Do you do maintenance contracts?

Yes. Under contract we inspect intruder alarms, fire alarms, CCTV and fire extinguishers every year, and send a detailed engineer’s report after each service.

Do you work with businesses?

Yes, from shops and workshops to a regional distribution warehouse. We install and maintain intruder alarms, CCTV, fire detection and access control for commercial and industrial premises as well as homes.

What areas do you cover?

We’re based in Leixlip, Co. Kildare. Our customers are in Clonsilla, Lucan, Donaghmede, Sutton and Co. Meath, and we work throughout the country.

How much does a system cost?

It depends on the property and what you need. Call 01 615 5980 or send a request and we’ll give you a competitive quote.
