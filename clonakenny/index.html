From the kitchen
to the field

In 2020 Ali was head chef at Cloughjordan House, at home with her third baby, when lockdown closed the kitchen. Kenny had put up a polytunnel for vegetables. With Ali's mum, Liz, they filled it with flowers instead.

Today the farm is about two acres of meadow and raised beds and four and a half polytunnels. Every flower starts as a seed here. Ali still works like a chef: use what's in season, at its best.

    1. February to March

      Sowing

      Seeds go in under the polytunnels. Nothing to cut yet, plenty to plan. A good time to book summer weddings.

    2. April to May

      First flowers

      The tunnels give the first cut flowers of the year, soft and early.

    3. June to July

      High summer

      The meadow and raised beds come in. Bouquets get bigger, brighter and looser.

    4. August to October

      Dahlia season

      The flower Clonakenny is known for, at its peak until the first frosts.

    5. November to December

      Dried and foraged

      Flowers dried on the farm, with foraged foliage, berries, twigs and dried wheat, go into December wreaths.
