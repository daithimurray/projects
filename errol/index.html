Things people ask before they book

Do I need to move my furniture?
No. Errol moves everything to the middle of the room, sheets it, and puts it back at the end of each day. Very large or fragile pieces get agreed at the quote stage.
How long does a room take?
One day for walls and ceiling in a standard room, plus half a day for woodwork. A hall, stairs and landing usually takes two to three days.
Which paints do you use?
Dulux Trade or Fleetwood as standard. Colourtrend, Farrow & Ball and Little Greene on request, with the difference priced on the quote.
Do you paint outside in winter?
Masonry paint needs a dry wall and temperatures above 8 °C, so exterior work runs from about April to October. Errol holds a week for you and confirms it against the forecast.
Which areas do you cover?
Leixlip, Celbridge, Maynooth, Lucan and Kilcock, and anywhere within about 20 km. Bigger jobs further out are fine, just ask.
