Common questions

Before you ring

Do you cover my area?

The work is based around Leixlip, Celbridge, Maynooth and the surrounding parts of North Kildare. If you are not sure whether your area is covered, call Ger and ask — it takes a moment.

How do I get a price?

Describe the job on the phone. Ger will tell you what is involved and what happens next. Some jobs need a look in person before anyone can give you a sensible figure.

Can I send the details instead of calling?

Yes. Use the quote form above. Include the town and a sentence about what is happening, and leave a phone number so Ger can come back to you.

Who actually does the work?

Ger. He takes the call, looks at the job and does or manages the work himself.
