Privacy Policy

What this website collects

This website has one form: the quote request on the contact page. It collects your name, phone number, the town or area the job is in, the type of work you need, and — if you choose to give them — your email address and a description of the job.

Nothing else is collected. There are no accounts, no payments and no tracking cookies set by this website.

Why it is collected

So that Ger can answer your enquiry and, if you go ahead, carry out the work. That is the only purpose.

How it is used and stored

Your details are sent to Ger by [FORM PROVIDER TO BE NAMED BEFORE LAUNCH] and are used to contact you about your enquiry. They are not sold, and they are not used for marketing unless you ask to be contacted that way.

How long it is kept

Enquiry details are kept for as long as they are needed to deal with your enquiry and any work that follows, and for as long as tax and business records require. [CONFIRM THE RETENTION PERIOD BEFORE LAUNCH.]

Your rights

You can ask what information is held about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. To make a request, ring the number on this website or write to [CONTACT ADDRESS FOR PRIVACY REQUESTS].

If you are not satisfied with the response, you can contact the Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie).

Cookies

This website sets no cookies of its own and loads no third-party trackers. If analytics is added later, this section — and the need for a consent control — must be reviewed first.
