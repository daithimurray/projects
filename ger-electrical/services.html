Electrical repairs

The problem

A socket has stopped working. A light will not come on. A switch has started behaving oddly, or something has been "grand for now" for a bit too long.

What Ger does

Looks at what is actually wrong before touching anything, tells you what is involved, and repairs it. If a repair is not the right answer, you will be told that too.

Fault finding

The problem

Something keeps tripping and you cannot work out why. Lights flicker. A circuit drops out now and again, usually at the worst moment. Nobody has been able to tell you the cause.

What Ger does

Traces the fault to its source rather than swapping parts and hoping. You get told what caused it and what it takes to fix it, before the work starts.

Call Ger about a fault

New sockets

The problem

Not enough sockets, or none in the right places. Extension leads running across the floor. A room that was never wired for the way you use it now.

What Ger does

Adds sockets where you want them, and talks through the sensible positions before anything is cut or drilled.

Lighting
