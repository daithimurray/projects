What happens next

A simple, honest process

  1. You send the enquiry

    Use the form, call us, or email — whatever is easiest.

  2. We come back to you

    We contact you to arrange a time that suits.

  3. We hold a 20-minute introductory call

    We talk about your business, your current accounting support and any problems you want to solve.

  4. We both decide if it is a fit

    If it is, we agree the services and the monthly fee. If not, no harm done.
