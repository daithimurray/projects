Services

Everything your business needs, in one place

From annual compliance to regular administration, ongoing reporting and support for business decisions — we cover the full accounting picture for small businesses.

Service 1

Accounts and tax

Your year-end obligations, handled — and explained in plain English so you always know what you owe and why.

What is included:

  • Annual accounts
  • Tax returns — corporation tax and income tax
  • VAT returns and registrations
  • Tax planning
  • Revenue correspondence

Who it suits: every business — sole traders and limited companies alike. If you dread year-end, do not know what tax bill is coming, or want your filings done right and on time, this is the foundation.

Bookkeeping

Accurate, up-to-date books without the evening and weekend admin.

What is included:

  • Transaction recording

Payroll

Your team paid correctly and on time, with Revenue kept up to date — without payroll taking over your week.

  • Employee changes — starters, leavers and updates

Service 4

Management accounts

Regular reports that turn your raw numbers into a clear picture of the business — not just once a year, but when decisions are being made.

What your reports help you understand:

  • Revenue — where it is coming from
  • Costs — where the money is going
  • Profit — what is actually left
  • Cash flow — what is safe to spend
