About us

The kind of accountant we would want

Liffey Accountancy exists because small businesses deserve an accountant who answers the phone, explains the numbers, and helps during the year, not just at year-end.

Our founder

Aoife Byrne

Aoife founded Liffey Accountancy in Maynooth in 2017, after 12 years in larger accountancy firms, where she kept seeing small business owners pay for compliance work but get little help actually running their businesses. The practice was set up to close that gap.

Our team

A five-person team

Today Liffey Accountancy is a team of five, grown mainly through referrals and repeat business. When you work with us, you deal with people who know your business, not a call centre or a rotating cast of juniors.

How we work

What working with us looks like

The same principles apply to every client:

  • Plain English: we explain your accounts and tax position until they make sense.
  • Answers within one working day for normal questions.
  • Agreed monthly fees: you know the cost before the work starts.
  • Cloud accounting and digital processes where practical.
  • Ongoing support during the year, with in-person meetings always an option.
Where we are going

A local practice, by choice

We are not trying to become a national firm. We are trying to be the best accountant a North Kildare business can have. That means staying close to our clients, keeping the team tight, and growing the way we always have: by doing good work that people recommend.
