Privacy Policy

This website collects nothing except what you type into the enquiry form and send yourself.

What this website collects

The enquiry form on the Contact page asks for your name, phone number, town or area, and the type of work you need. An email address and a message are optional.

Nothing else is collected. There is no cookie banner because the site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from a third-party server. No tracking pixels, no advertising tags, no embedded maps or fonts from elsewhere.

Why it is collected

So Ger can reply to your enquiry and quote the job. That is the only purpose.

How your enquiry is sent

Pressing "Send enquiry" opens the message in your own email app with the details already filled in. Nothing leaves your device until you press send there, and the details do not pass through a third-party form service.

How it is used

  • To ring or email you back about the work you described.
  • To arrange a visit and quote the job.
  • To keep a record of work carried out, where one is needed.

Your details are not sold, shared for marketing, or added to a mailing list. Ger will not send you anything you did not ask for.

How long it is kept

Enquiries that do not lead to work are deleted once it is clear nothing is going ahead. Where work is carried out, the contact details and job record are kept for as long as Ger's tax and insurance obligations require.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask what information Ger holds about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. Ring 087 000 0000 or email enquiries@example.ie and say what you want done.

If you are not happy with the response, you can complain to the Data Protection Commission at dataprotection.ie.

Changes to this policy

If the website ever adds analytics or a hosted form service, this page will be updated to say so before that change goes live.

Who is responsible

Ger, Electrician, North Kildare. Contact details as above.
