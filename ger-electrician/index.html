    Electrical repairs

The person who turns up

Ger is a one-man operation. He quotes the job, he does the job, and he is the person you ring afterwards if something is not right. There is no call centre and no rotating crew.

He has been wiring and repairing houses in this part of Kildare for around thirty years, which means most faults he meets are ones he has met before.

Work is left tidy and tested, and he will tell you plainly if a job is bigger than you were expecting — or smaller.
