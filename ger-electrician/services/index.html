What each job involves

02.1

Electrical repairs in North Kildare

When you would call
A socket has stopped working. The immersion will not heat. An outside light died after a storm. A switch is warm, scorched or crackling.
What is involved
Ger tests the circuit, finds what has actually failed, and repairs or replaces it. Where a part has to be ordered in, he will tell you before he leaves rather than after.

Call Ger about a repair — 087 000 0000

02.2

Fault finding

When you would call
The RCD trips every time you use one appliance. Half the house has gone dead. The lights dim when the shower runs.
What is involved
Diagnostic work: isolating circuits, testing, and narrowing the cause down before anything is replaced. It is the difference between fixing the fault and swapping parts until the symptom goes away.

02.3

Sockets and switches

When you would call
You are running the kitchen off an extension lead. A socket is cracked, loose or browned. You want a point in the garage, the garden or behind a new television.
What is involved
New sockets added to an existing circuit, damaged accessories replaced, and outdoor-rated points where the cable run allows it.

Call Ger — 087 000 0000

02.4

Lighting

When you would call
New ceiling lights going in, downlighters that have started failing one by one, under-unit lighting for a kitchen, or a security light that never worked properly.
What is involved
Supply and fit, or fit-only if you have already bought the fittings. Switching changed or added where the existing wiring allows it.

Call Ger — 087 000 0000

02.5

Upgrades

When you would call
An old rewireable fuse board. A board with no RCD protection. A circuit that trips whenever the kettle and the toaster are on together.
What is involved
Replacing a consumer unit, adding protection, or upgrading a circuit that is carrying more than it was built for. Ger will look at what is already there and tell you what actually needs doing, rather than replacing the lot.

Call Ger — 087 000 0000

02.6

Maintenance

When you would call
You own a rental and want the electrics looked at between tenancies. You manage property and need one electrician you can ring.
What is involved
Inspection and repair of the existing installation, with a plain note of anything found and what it would take to put it right.

02.7

Small installations

When you would call
An extension, attic conversion or garden room that needs power and light. A new oven, hob or electric shower going in.
What is involved
New circuits run from the board, with the board itself checked to make sure it can carry them.
Worth knowing
This is installation work on existing homes rather than full rewires or new builds. Ring and describe the job and Ger will tell you straight away whether it is one for him.
