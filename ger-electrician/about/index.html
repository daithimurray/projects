01

In Ger's own words

I started in the trade around thirty years ago and I have worked in this part of Kildare for most of it. After that long in the same few towns, most faults I get called to are ones I have seen before.

Nearly all my work comes from someone passing on my number. That is the part of the job I want to keep, so I would rather turn a job down than do it badly or leave you waiting on me.

I work on my own. When you ring, you get me. When I quote, I am the one who will be doing the work. And if something is not right afterwards, you ring the same number.

Most of what I do is domestic — repairs, fault finding, sockets and lighting, older boards brought up to date, and small installations for extensions and garden rooms. I also look after rental properties for landlords and property managers.

— Ger
