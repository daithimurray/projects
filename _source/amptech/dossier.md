## 1. Executive Summary

- Amptech is a small intruder alarm, CCTV and fire system installer in Leixlip, Co. Kildare.
- The legal entity is **Robert Farnan Electrical & Alarms Limited** (CRO 501850). "Amptech" is a trading name. (Verified via PSA register)
- The company holds PSA licence **03019**. The licence covers intruder alarms, CCTV and access control. It is valid to 12/04/2028. (Verified)
- The current website has 7 main pages. It has short copy, 6 testimonials, and phone-first calls to action.
- The site says "over 20 years" of experience. The company was incorporated in 2011. The earlier history is not documented. (Unverified)
- Main gaps for the redesign:
  - The privacy policy names the controller as "Amptech Ltd". No Irish company with that name was found. The real entity is Robert Farnan Electrical & Alarms Limited.
  - Access control is licensed but not marketed anywhere on the site.
  - No public review profile with volume was found.
  - No named team, no email address on the site, and no service-area pages.
  - Several copy errors, including a wrong standard number ("EN5013-1").

## 4. Services

Use these exact service names from the current navigation: Security, Maintenance, System Upgrades, Monitoring Services.

**4.1 Security (installation)** — https://www.amptech.ie/security/
- Intruder alarm systems for domestic, industrial and commercial sites.
- Wired and wireless intruder alarms.
- Alarm monitoring and GSM text monitoring.
- Emergency 24/7 call-out service, 365 days a year. (Company claim)
- Completion certificate issued after each installation.
- Stated standard: "EN5013-1". The correct standard is probably EN 50131-1. (Inferred typo — confirm with client)
- Claim: "AmpTech can repair & upgrade any intruder alarm system."

**4.2 Maintenance** — https://www.amptech.ie/maintenance/
- Fully contracted maintenance service.
- Annual inspection of: intruder alarms, fire alarms, CCTV systems, fire extinguishers.
- A detailed engineer's report after each service.

**4.3 System Upgrades** — https://www.amptech.ie/system-upgrades/
- Replace outdated intruder, CCTV, fire detection and lighting systems.
- Add zones after extensions or home changes.
- Uniquely numbered "Certificate of Compliance" for intruder systems. The site says insurers may ask for it. It may help get a premium discount.
- Fire detection: review, design and upgrades to bring premises "up to code".
- CCTV upgrades to high-resolution, low-light cameras.

**4.4 Monitoring Services** — https://www.amptech.ie/monitoring-services/
- 24/7 monitored systems.
- Systems can get a Garda Unique Reference Number (URN). This can qualify for a Level One Garda response to a confirmed alarm. (Company claim)
- Designated keyholders can be called.
- Amptech's PSA licence does NOT include the "Alarm Monitoring" category. Monitoring is probably provided by a third-party monitoring centre. The partner is not named. (Inferred — confirm with client)

**4.5 Licensed but not marketed**
- **Access control.** The PSA licence includes "Installer of Security Equipment – Access Control". The website does not mention it. (Verified gap)

**4.6 Legacy services (possibly discontinued)**
- An old directory copy of the site lists "shower installations", "electrical installations" and "remote monitoring". (Reported — irelands-advisor.com)
- The Facebook page describes "Electronic Security & Electrical installation & Services". (Reported)
- Confirm with client if general electrical work is still offered.
