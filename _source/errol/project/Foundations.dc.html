Responsive behaviour

  • Display and headings scale fluidly via clamp; body text never changes size.
  • Below 640px, Display caps at 44px and line-height stays 1.0 to avoid 4-line heroes.
  • Measure: 60–72 characters. Cap paragraphs at max-width: 65ch.
  • Use text-wrap: balance on headings, pretty on body.

Accessibility

  • Root font size stays at the user’s setting; all sizes in rem (spec shows px equivalents).
  • Body 16px minimum; Small 14px only for supporting metadata, never for instructions.
  • Text must reflow to 320px wide and zoom to 200% without horizontal scroll.
  • One h1 per page; heading levels never skip. Visual level and semantic level may differ via class.
  • Caption caps: 0.1em tracking keeps uppercase readable; never use caps above 14px.

Weights and italics

  • Newsreader: 500 default, 600 for pull quotes, 400 italic for testimonials only.
  • Figtree: 400 body, 500 labels and nav, 600 buttons and H4, 700 numerals in stats.
  • Never fake bold or italic. Load only the listed weights (5 files, ~110 KB).
  • Tabular numerals (font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums) in price tables.

Calm and quick. Motion confirms a tap or reveals a panel; it never performs.

Respect prefers-reduced-motion: fade only, no transforms.

No bounce, spring, or overshoot. Entrances: opacity 0→1 plus translateY 8px→0. Exits are faster than entrances (÷1.5). Before/after slider follows the pointer with no easing.

Icons & imagery

Icons

  • Lucide, 24px grid, 1.75px stroke, round caps. Inline SVG with currentColor.
  • Sizes: 16 (inline with Small text), 20 (buttons, inputs), 24 (nav, feature rows). Never larger than 32 as UI; larger is illustration.
  • Decorative icons get aria-hidden; standalone icon buttons get a visible tooltip and aria-label.
  • No emoji in UI. No icon fonts.
