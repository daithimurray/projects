Rules

  • Details last: people give contact info once they’ve invested.
  • Every step skippable except the last; skipped items show as “We’ll ask” in the summary.
  • Final button is the only Ochre button in the flow.
  • Confirmation page repeats the summary and the reply time (“by Tuesday”).

Validation

  • Inline on blur; summary Alert on submit with links to fields.
  • Eircode checked live against the service area; out-of-area shows an Info alert with the nearest we cover, not a dead end.
  • Draft saved to local storage; “Leave without sending?” Modal guards navigation.

6 · Voice & copy

A tradesperson who explains things properly. Plain, specific, a bit dry. “We” is Errol (and whoever’s on the job that day); “you” is the client.

Rules

  • Sentence case everywhere. Product/paint names keep their capitals.
  • Headlines ≤ 8 words, say what changes for the client.
  • Buttons: verb + object. “Get a quote”, “Book a visit”, “See our work”.
  • Prices: “from €480” lowercase from, per unit stated, VAT status once per page (confirm whether registered).
  • Time: “within one working day”, “w/c 12 Oct”. Never “ASAP” or “soon”.
  • No exclamation marks. One emoji, nowhere.
  • Contractions yes; jargon no (say “low-sheen”, then “eggshell” in brackets).
