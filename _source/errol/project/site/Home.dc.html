Questions

Things people ask before they book

Something else? Ring 087 875 3433 and Errol will answer.

Do I need to move my furniture?

No. We move everything to the middle of the room, sheet it, and put it back at the end of each day. Very large or fragile pieces we’ll agree at the quote stage.

How long does a room take?

One day for walls and ceiling in a standard room; add half a day for woodwork. Hall, stairs and landing is usually two to three days.

Which paints do you use?

Dulux Trade or Fleetwood as standard. Colourtrend, Farrow & Ball and Little Greene on request, with the difference priced on the quote.

Do you do exterior work in winter?

Masonry paint needs a dry surface and above 8 °C, so exteriors run roughly April to October. We’ll hold a week and confirm on the forecast.

Which areas do you cover?

Leixlip, Celbridge, Maynooth, Lucan and Kilcock, and anywhere within about 20 km. Further out is fine for bigger jobs, just ask.
