Services

What we paint

Homes first, small commercial when it suits. Every job gets the same prep, the same two coats, and the same tidy-up.

Interior

Walls, ceilings & woodwork

Cracks filled and sanded flush, stains sealed, edges cut in by hand.

  • Full room, single wall, or the whole house
  • Skirting, doors, architrave and window boards in satin or eggshell
  • Ceilings and cornices, including stain-blocking after leaks
  • Furniture moved, covered and returned each evening
Get a quoteFrom €480 per room, paint included.

Exterior

Render, dash, fascia & doors

We stabilise cracks, treat algae, and use a masonry paint rated for Irish weather, so it still looks good after the second winter.

  • Pebble-dash and smooth render, front-only or whole house
  • Fascia, soffit, gutters and downpipes
  • Front doors, garage doors, gates and railings
  • Weather-watched scheduling, April to October
Get a quoteFrom €1,900 for a front elevation.

Decorating

Wallpaper & feature walls

Pattern-matched, seams kept out of sight, and lining paper first where the wall needs it.

  • Hanging paste-the-wall and traditional papers
  • Murals and wide-format panels
  • Stripping and making good before repainting
  • Colour advice if you want a second opinion
Get a quoteFrom €320 per wall, paper supplied by you or us.

Shops, offices & rentals

Out-of-hours and turnaround work

Evenings and weekends for shops and practices, fast turnarounds between tenants for landlords.

  • Scrubbable, low-odour paints for busy spaces
  • Evening and weekend slots around your opening hours
  • Rental refresh: walls, ceilings, woodwork in one visit
  • One invoice, itemised, VAT status confirmed on the quote
