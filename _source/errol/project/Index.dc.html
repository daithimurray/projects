Principles

Five rules every screen answers to

01

Clean lines

A decorator is judged on edges. Straight alignment, consistent gutters, one accent at a time. Nothing bleeds.

02

Show the work

Photography of finished rooms carries the brand. UI stays quiet so the work is loud. No decoration competes with a photo.

03

Quote in three taps

Every page can reach “Get a quote” or “Call” within one thumb move. Forms ask only what a decorator needs to price a job.

04

Legible on site

Read in daylight, on a dusty phone, with gloves off for a second. 16px minimum body, 4.5:1 contrast, 48px targets.

05

Honest and local

Real names, real towns, real prices where possible. Plain English, sentence case, no jargon. We say “we”, and we mean Errol.

Do’s and don’ts

The short list

Do
  • Use one Ochre element per viewport: the primary action or one highlight.
  • Set headings in Newsreader; everything interactive in Figtree.
  • Put a phone number and “Get a quote” in the header on every page.
  • Show before/after photos at the same crop and aspect ratio.
  • Write prices as “from €480” with what’s included underneath.
  • Keep body text at 16px or larger, 60–72 characters per line.
  • Use warm Plaster neutrals for surfaces; pure white only for cards.
Don’t
  • Don’t put Ochre text on white below 24px; it fails 4.5:1. Use Ochre 700.
  • Don’t stack gradients, drop shadows, and borders on one card. Pick one.
  • Don’t use stock photos of “painters” smiling at rollers. Use Errol’s jobs.
  • Don’t use Title Case in headings or buttons. Sentence case only.
  • Don’t hide the price behind “Contact us” when a range is known.
  • Don’t use icons as the only label. Text first, icon optional.
  • Don’t animate anything longer than 400ms or with a bounce.
