Privacy

This site is a single page about my books and readings. It is built to collect as little as possible.

What this site stores

  • No cookies, no analytics, no advertising or tracking scripts.
  • If you switch between the light and dark theme, your choice is saved in your own browser (local storage) so the page remembers it. It never leaves your device, and clearing your browser data removes it.

If you write to me or join the letter

Where the page offers a contact form or a sign-up for the letter, what you enter (your name, email address and message) is sent to the service that delivers it to me, and is used only to reply to you or to send the letter. You can unsubscribe from the letter at any time using the link in every email, or ask me to delete your details.

Links to other sites

Links to bookshops, publishers, festivals and X take you to other sites with their own privacy policies.

Questions

Contact me through the contact section of the main page.
