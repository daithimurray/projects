Cathy Conlon: short biography

Cathy Conlon is an Irish writer living in Celbridge, Co. Kildare. She writes poetry, short stories, radio drama and fiction. Some earlier work appeared under her full name, Catherine Conlon.

Her debut poetry collection, The Light Dancing, was published by Revival Press in 2025. Her first novel, Swift, Vanessa & The Sluttery, was published by Savoy Editions and launched at Barberstown Castle by Ger Duffy in May 2026. Two of her stage plays have been performed, and a radio play was shortlisted for the RTÉ P.J. O'Connor Awards.

Her poems and stories have appeared in The Irish Times, Poetry Ireland Review, Books Ireland, Cúirt Journal, Ropes, Skylight 47 and Boyne Berries.
