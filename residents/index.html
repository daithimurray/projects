What we do

Three jobs, done by neighbours.

  1. 01

    A voice with the council.

    We raise issues on roads, lighting, green spaces and planning with the council and local reps.

  2. 02

    Events that bring people together.

    Clean-ups, summer fun days, Halloween trails and the Christmas lights switch-on.

  3. 03

    Information you can trust.

    Meeting minutes, updates and local notices, all in one place.
