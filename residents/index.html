Looking after Barnhall Meadows, together.

What taking in charge means

3 of 5 steps done. Waiting on the council’s decision. Latest update we have found: September 2025.

  1. April 2018. Done.

    Planning permission granted

    An Bord Pleanála approved up to 450 homes, a crèche and open space.

    Source: An Bord Pleanála, ABP-300606

  2. From 2020. Done.

    Residents move in

    Until the handover, the developer looks after the roads, footpaths, street lights and green areas.

  3. Nov to Dec 2024. Done.

    Council consultation

    Kildare County Council asked the public for views on taking the estate in charge.

    Source: Kildare County Council consultation

  4. 4

    Waiting. In progress.

    Council decision

    Still not taken in charge at the last update we found. We are asking the council for the current position.

    Source: Kildare Now, September 2025

  5. 5

    Date not set. Not started.

    The council takes over

    The council maintains the roads, footpaths, street lights, open spaces and drains.

The estate

Living beside the Wonderful Barn.

Barnhall Meadows was built by Glenveagh Homes on the former Wonderful Barn lands, off the Celbridge Road (R404). Planning permission for up to 450 homes, a crèche, open space and a new roundabout on the R404 was granted in April 2018.

The estate takes its name from Barnhall House and the Wonderful Barn beside it. Katherine Conolly of Castletown built the barn in 1743, probably as famine relief work. Its corkscrew stair winds around the outside, between two conical dovecotes.

Owners, private renters and Clúid Housing tenants all live here. The association is for all of them.

Sources: An Bord Pleanála case ABP-300606; Glenveagh: Barnhall Meadows; Clúid Housing: Barnhall Meadows; The Wonderful Barn (Wikipedia)
