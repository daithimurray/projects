Who to contact for what

  • Emergencies: call 999 or 112.

  • Roads, street lights and green areas: the estate is not yet taken in charge, so these are still the developer’s job. Report them to Glenveagh Homes (contact to be confirmed) and copy us.

  • Bins, illegal dumping and other council services: report to Kildare County Council (reporting link to be confirmed).

  • Clúid Housing tenants: report repairs in your home to Clúid Housing.

  • Anti-social behaviour: Leixlip Garda Station, 19 Station Road, Leixlip, 01 666 7800.

  • Everything else: use the form.
