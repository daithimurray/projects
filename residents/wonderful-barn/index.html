About this model

This model follows the 2024 measured survey of the barn. Metropolitan Workshop made the survey for Kildare County Council, for the Part 8 plan (reference P82024.10).

Measured from the survey drawings

  • Floor levels, dome heights and the total height (21.6 m above the ground floor).
  • The slope of the cone, the wall thickness and the size of each room.
  • The four arms, the doors and windows, and the route of the 94-step stair.
  • The pigeon houses, yard walls, Barnhall House and the stables, in plan and in height.

Taken from photographs and maps

  • Materials: rubble stone, lime render and hung slate, from drone photographs in the Barnhall Meadows brochure.
  • Roads, house outlines and the M4, from the survey location maps and a satellite view.

Estimated

  • Where the dome curves start, the position of the date-stone, and the interior finishes.
  • Barnhall House in 1743, and the details of the 2024 plan.
  • The River Liffey, the business campus and the far-away landmarks.
  • Trees: six Irish species grown by the model (oak, beech, ash, sycamore, hawthorn and birch). Their places follow the satellite view, but each tree's shape is made up.
  • Homes: each surveyed home stands on its mapped outline. The house types, colours, gardens and cars are typical, not surveyed. The estates beyond the survey maps are generated.
  • Footpaths and kerbs: a standard width inside each mapped road.
