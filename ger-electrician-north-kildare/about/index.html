About Ger

One electrician, working on his own, in the same corner of Kildare he has worked in for most of his career.

Thirty years of the same trade

Ger has been an electrician for around thirty years. He works for himself, does the work himself, and answers his own phone. There is no office between you and him, and no chance of a different person turning up than the one you spoke to.

Most of that work has been domestic: houses in Leixlip, Celbridge, Maynooth and the towns around them. Over that length of time in one area you learn how the local housing stock was built and wired, which estates share the same recurring faults, and what is usually behind a problem before the cover comes off. That is the part that saves a customer money.

Registered and insured

Ger is a Registered Electrical Contractor with the Safe Electric scheme, and he is fully insured for the work he carries out. If you want to see either of those confirmed before he starts, ask him on the phone. He would rather you asked.

How he works

He looks at the job before quoting for it. He tells you what needs doing now and what can wait, which is not always the same list. He tests what he has worked on before he leaves. If a job turns out to be bigger than it looked, you hear about it at that point, not at the end.

Small jobs are welcome. A single dead socket is a normal call, not an inconvenience.

Who he works for

Mostly homeowners. Also landlords who need the electrics kept right between tenancies, and small local businesses for straightforward maintenance and installation work.
