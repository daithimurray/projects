Electrical repairs in North Kildare

A socket has gone dead. The immersion has stopped heating. A breaker keeps tripping and nobody knows why. Something that used to work does not work now.

Ger repairs domestic wiring, sockets, switches, light fittings, circuits and consumer units. He tests the circuit before and after so you know the repair actually holds rather than lasting a fortnight.

Call Ger about a repair

Fault finding

The hardest electrical problems are the ones that come and go. The trip happens on wet days. The lights dim when the shower runs. Someone has already been out twice and it is still doing it.

Fault finding is a process of elimination with proper test equipment, not guesswork. Ger isolates circuits, tests them individually, and tells you what he found and what it will take to put right before he starts the repair.

Call Ger about a fault

Sockets and switches

Most houses around here were wired for far fewer appliances than they now run. Extension leads daisy chained behind the telly are the usual sign.

Ger fits additional sockets where you actually use them, replaces damaged or scorched fittings, adds outdoor and garage points, and swaps switches. Where a circuit is already near its limit he will say so rather than adding to it.

Call Ger about sockets

Lighting

New light fittings, replacing what is there, or sorting out lights that flicker, buzz, or were never wired correctly in the first place.

Indoor and outdoor lighting, kitchen and bathroom fittings, security lights, and switch positions moved to where they make sense. If you have bought a fitting yourself, Ger will tell you before he starts whether it is suitable for where you want it.

Call Ger about lighting

Electrical upgrades

Older houses were wired to a different standard and for a different amount of electricity. Rewireable fuses, no residual current protection, and boards with no spare ways are all common in the area.

Ger upgrades consumer units, adds protective devices, and brings older circuits up to how the house is actually used now. He will look at what is there first and tell you what genuinely needs doing and what can wait.

Call Ger about an upgrade

Electrical maintenance

For homeowners keeping on top of things, and for landlords who need the electrics in a rental property looked after between tenancies.

Checking and replacing worn fittings, testing protective devices, and dealing with the small things before they turn into a callout. Ger will give you a plain list of what he found and what he did.

Call Ger about maintenance

Small installations

New circuits and fixed appliances: cookers, showers, immersions, extractor fans, garden and shed supplies.

Each one is put in on a suitable circuit, protected correctly, and tested before Ger leaves. Where an installation needs more capacity than the existing board can give, he will tell you that at the quote stage rather than halfway through the job.
