What Ger does

Domestic electrical work, small jobs and large. If your job is not listed, it is still worth ringing to ask.

Electrical repairs Something has stopped working, keeps tripping, or was never right. Ger finds the cause and puts it back in order. The man who answers the phone is the man who does the work

Ger has worked as an electrician for around thirty years, most of it in the same few towns. There is no office, no call centre and no rotating crew. You ring him, he comes out, and he is the one standing in your kitchen.

That matters more than it sounds. He has seen how the houses around here were wired, which estates have the same recurring problems, and what tends to be behind a fault before he has the cover off.
