Privacy Policy

This site is run by Ger, an independent electrician working in North Kildare. This page explains what happens to any information you send.

What the form collects

The quote request form on the contact page asks for your name, phone number, town or area, and the type of work you need. Your email address and any extra detail about the job are optional.

How it reaches Ger

The form does not send anything by itself. When you press Request a Quote, your own email app opens with the details already written into a message addressed to Ger. Nothing leaves your device until you press send in that app. There is no database behind this site and no third party form service handling your enquiry.

Why it is collected and how it is used

The information is used for one thing: so Ger can get back to you about the work you asked about, and carry it out if you go ahead. It is not used for marketing, it is not sold, and it is not shared with anyone else except where he is legally required to share it.

How long it is kept

Enquiries stay in Ger's email account. Records relating to work actually carried out are kept for as long as tax and safety obligations require, and enquiries that do not lead to work are deleted once they are no longer needed.

Cookies and analytics

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking scripts. The font used on these pages is served from this site itself, so no request is made to any outside company when you visit.

Your rights

Under data protection law you can ask what information Ger holds about you, ask him to correct it, or ask him to delete it. Ring him on 087 000 0000 or email enquiries@example.ie and say what you would like done.

If you are not happy with how a request was handled, you can complain to the Data Protection Commission at dataprotection.ie.
