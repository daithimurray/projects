05 · Questions

Before we meet

How does the free first consultation work?+

At this meeting we seek to understand your business, the range of services you require, and to identify the most appropriate solutions to your requirements. There is no charge.

Can I end the Financial Controller service?+

Yes. Contracts operate on a month-to-month basis, so you can switch on or switch off your agreement with us as you see fit.

Do you act for Irish contractors based in the UK?+

Yes. We are well informed on the issues that arise when moving from one tax jurisdiction to another, and act for clients whose affairs are dealt with by HMRC.

Why outsource rather than employ a Financial Controller?+

Many small businesses need a Financial Controller for a few days a month but cannot justify a full-time hire. Outsourcing removes the employment contract, holiday pay and unfair-dismissal exposure, and saves the employer's PRSI contribution.
