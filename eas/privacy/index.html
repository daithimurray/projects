How we handle the details you send through this website.

Who we are

Everyday Accountancy Services Limited (company no. 606041), registered office 8 Eglinton Street, Galway, is responsible for the personal data described here. Contact us at info@everydayaccountancy.ie or on (091) 547 525.

What this website collects

  • This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking.
  • Fonts are served from this website, not from a third party.
  • The enquiry form does not send anything by itself. It opens your own email app with a message addressed to us. Nothing reaches us unless you choose to send that email.

What we do with your enquiry

When you email or call us, we use your name, contact details and anything you tell us about your business to reply and to arrange a first meeting. We do not use it for marketing and we do not sell or share it with third parties for their own use.

If you do not become a client, we delete your enquiry when it is no longer needed to respond to you. If you become a client, your information is handled under the terms of our engagement with you.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing info@everydayaccountancy.ie. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Data Protection Commission at dataprotection.ie.
